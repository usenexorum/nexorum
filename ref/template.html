Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Email hello@nexorum.io.

How long does a project take?
Most logo and branding projects take three to six weeks. Packaging and UI/UX work depend on scope, and you'll get a timeline before we start.
What do you need from me to get started?
A short brief: what you do, who it's for, and any references you like or dislike. I'll send a questionnaire to fill in the gaps.
How many revisions are included?
Two rounds of refinement are included at each stage. Extra rounds can be added if the project needs them.
Which files will I receive?
Print and screen-ready files (SVG, PDF and PNG), the source files, and simple guidelines for using them.
Do you work with clients remotely?
Yes. Everything from kickoff to handover can happen online, across time zones.

Ready for a logo or design that truly represents your brand?

Tell me a little about your project and I'll reply within two working days.
