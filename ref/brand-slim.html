Clear space

Give it room

Keep one sixth of the logo's width free on every side. Nothing else enters that zone.

Background

Black first

The mark lives on pure black or the liquid-chrome texture. Never place it on light or busy photos.

Wordmark

Name to the right

NEXORUM in Archivo ExtraBold Italic, cap height equal to half the square, spaced one third of the square away.

The coin

The 3D coin is the identity's hero object. Use it for launches, social posts and anywhere the brand needs a single memorable image.

Shape language

Everything leans the same way. Interactive elements borrow the N's slant; containers borrow the logo's cut corners.

The 20° slant

Taken from the N's strokes. Used for ticks, bands and motion paths.

Cut corners

Top-left and bottom-right trimmed on every card, panel and frame.

Start a project

Parallelograms

Buttons, tags and chips are skewed −14°, never rounded pills.

The sparkle

A four-point chrome star. One large and one small per composition, no more.

Do and don't

A few rules that keep the mark looking expensive.

✓

On black

Pure black or the chrome texture only.

✕

On light colors

The square turns into a sticker. Don't.

✕

Rotated or skewed

The N already leans. The square stays upright.

✕

Recolored

No gold, no color tints. Chrome only.
